FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

We start from 500 pieces per project. The exact MOQ depends on product, material and finishing.

Do I get a sample before the series?

Yes. Before every series production we develop a sample for you to review and approve. Sampling costs and duration depend on the project.

How long does production take?

That depends on quantity, product and material. After reviewing your project you receive a concrete delivery window.

Which finishing options do you offer?

Among others embroidery, screen printing, DTF and DTG, plus private label elements such as labels, hangtags and packaging.

Where is production based?

Production is focused in Turkey with selected partner factories. MEINTEX handles project management, quality control and logistics to Germany and the EU.

Are very large quantities possible?

Yes, we accept enquiries up to 500,000+ pieces. We plan large series and framework projects after an individual capacity review.
